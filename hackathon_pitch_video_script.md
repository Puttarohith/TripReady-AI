## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide: How to Generate & Assemble Your Video

1. **Copy Each Prompt into Gemini / Video AI**:
   - Open Gemini (or your AI Video Generator like Imagen/Veo/Runway).
   - Prepend the **Master Visual Prompt Prefix** to each scene's video generation prompt to maintain character facial structure, hoodie, and art style across all 26 clips.
   - Generate each 8-second clip (total 26 clips).
2. **Record the Voiceover (VO)**:
   - Read the provided voiceover text or use an AI voice tool (e.g. ElevenLabs, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech) using a warm, confident, pacing-matched voice (~18 to 22 words per 8-second clip).
3. **Assemble in Video Editor (CapCut, Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve)**:
   - Place each 8-second clip in order on Track 1.
   - Add the voiceover audio on Audio Track 1.
   - Add subtle cinematic background music (lo-fi ambient or uplifting electronic build) on Audio Track 2 (volume at -18dB).
   - Add the provided **On-Screen Text (OST)** lower-thirds for judges to follow effortlessly.
4. **Final Export**:
   - 1080p or 4K, 60fps, MP4. Perfect 3m 28s duration for hackathon submission!
